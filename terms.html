1. agreeing to these terms

by adding hollow to a server, using its commands, or being in a server that uses it, you agree to these terms and to our privacy policy. if you don't agree, don't use hollow, and server owners can remove it at any time.

2. discord's rules come first

hollow runs on discord, so you also have to follow discord's terms of service and community guidelines. you must be old enough to use discord to use hollow.

3. what you can't do

  • use hollow to harass, threaten, dox or target anyone.
  • use hollow to spam, raid, mass ping or mass dm people.
  • post or spread illegal content, or content that sexualizes minors, through any hollow feature, including confessions, tags, autoresponders, embeds, suggestions and applications.
  • try to break, overload, exploit or get around hollow's limits, permission checks or safety features.
  • abuse bugs on purpose instead of reporting them with ,feedback.
  • use hollow to break copyright, for example reposting or playing content you don't have the right to share.
  • pretend to be hollow, its staff, or discord.
  • copy, resell or reverse engineer hollow.

4. server owners and staff

  • you're responsible for how hollow is set up and used in your server, including bans, kicks, timeouts, role changes, automod, anti raid and antinuke actions.
  • features like fake permissions let you hand mod commands to roles. only give them to people you trust. what they do with them is on the server, not on hollow.
  • nothing in hollow that kicks, bans or deletes turns on by itself. you choose what to turn on.
  • let your members know if you use features that store their data, like tickets, modmail or confessions.
  • hollow's role order, permission and rate limit checks are there to help, but you're still responsible for your server's own permissions.

5. your content

anything you put into hollow, like messages, embeds, tags, autoresponders, images or settings, stays yours. you give hollow permission to store and show it only so the features you use can work. you're responsible for what you put into hollow and must have the right to share it.

6. features that act for you

some features act automatically once a server turns them on, like automod deleting messages, anti raid kicking new accounts, antinuke stripping roles, giveaways picking winners, or alerts posting. results can be wrong sometimes. staff should check important actions, and we're not responsible for automatic actions a server chose to turn on.

giveaway prizes, shop items and economy money are handled by each server. hollow's economy money has no real world value and can't be sold or traded for real money.

7. free, as is

hollow is free. it's provided "as is" and "as available", without any promise that it will always be online, free of bugs, or that features won't change. we may add, change or remove features at any time. if paid features are ever added, they'll come with their own clear terms.

8. limits of responsibility

as far as the law allows, we're not responsible for any loss or damage from using or not being able to use hollow, including lost data, moderation actions, downtime, or content posted by other users. you use hollow at your own risk.

9. blocking and ending

  • we can block any user or server from using hollow, or remove hollow from a server, if these terms or discord's rules are broken, or to protect hollow and its users.
  • you can stop using hollow any time. server owners can remove it, and anyone can delete their data with ,deletedata as explained in the privacy policy.

10. changes

we may update these terms when hollow changes. the date at the top shows the latest version, and big changes are announced in hollow's update messages. using hollow after a change means you accept the new terms.
